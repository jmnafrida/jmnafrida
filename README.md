<h2 align="center">About Me</h2>
<img align="right" width="300" src="about-art.svg" alt="A girl in a white sweater coding at a curved monitor by a window over a night city">
<p>I moved to America with the hope of becoming an Accountant, but an on-campus job at the IT help desk showed me how much I enjoy solving technical problems. In my junior year, I switched my major to <b>Information Technology and Systems</b> and never looked back.</p>
<p>Since then, I've worked at the <b>Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas</b> as an <b>ITS Architecture Intern</b>, where I replaced a district-wide tracking platform by building a new one with Power Apps, Power Automate and Power BI on Azure, connecting SharePoint and SQL data pipelines and shipping it through Azure DevOps.</p>
<p>After graduation, I joined <b>CBRE</b>, where I work across the full data lifecycle. I've turned complex operational data into analysis and dashboards used across four regions, built data pipelines and ML models that guide repair-or-replace decisions, and created multi-agent LLM systems that flag work order escalations in real time, plus a hackathon-winning troubleshooting assistant for technicians.</p>
<p>I'm also doing my master's part time in <b>Data Science</b> at <b>Penn</b> to deepen my technical knowledge and keep growing my skill set.</p>
